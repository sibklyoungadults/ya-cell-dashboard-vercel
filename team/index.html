📖 How Portal Settings works

✅ Do it here

  • Show or hide public tabs: Choose which tabs leaders see on the public site. Team tabs, including Bookings by Date, are always available after logging in.
  • Rename tabs: Give any public or team tab a clearer name, emoji included.
  • Tab descriptions: Edit the short guide at the top of each tab.
  • Welcome note: A message shown on the public site under the tab bar. Leave it empty to hide it.
  • Collection details: What leaders see when they tap a booking in Cell Bookings, such as key, AV guide, aircon, instruments and signage.
  • WhatsApp reminders: Edit both templates and the AV guide link.
  • Custom tab: Switch on an extra public tab and write your own content.

💾 How saving works

  • Nothing is saved until you publish: Edits show on your screen right away, but only you see them.
  • Check first: 👁 Preview as leader opens the public site in a new tab with your unsaved edits.
  • Publish: Save changes → Confirm copies the settings. In GitHub, paste them into portal-config.json and commit.
  • Everyone updates: Vercel redeploys in about a minute, then everyone sees the change after a refresh.
  • Who can publish: Anyone with write access to the GitHub repo.
  • Don’t leave mid-edit: Closing or leaving the page discards unpublished edits.

↗️ Done outside the portal

  • Bookings: In Google Calendar. Title the event “YA Cell” and add one line per room in the description, e.g. M6-1: The Dwelling. Booked rooms with no line show as Unassigned.
  • Booking requests: In the Google Sheet the Request a Booking form writes to.
  • Team password: TEAM_PASSWORD in Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables, then redeploy.
  • Undo a change: In GitHub, open the history of portal-config.json and revert the commit.
  • Bigger changes: Ask Claude for layout, new features or new kinds of tabs.

⚠️ Settings are public: portal-config.json can be opened by anyone with the site link, even the team-tab text. Don’t put passwords or personal details in any field here.
