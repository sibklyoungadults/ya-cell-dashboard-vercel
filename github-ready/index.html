📖 How Portal Settings works

✅ Do it here

  • Show or hide tabs: Choose which tabs leaders see. Leaders are anyone without the passcode.
  • Rename tabs: Give any tab a clearer name, emoji included.
  • Tab descriptions: Edit the short guide at the top of each tab.
  • Welcome note: A message shown to everyone under the tab bar. Leave it empty to hide it.
  • Collection details: What leaders see when they tap a booking, such as key, AV guide, aircon, instruments and signage.
  • WhatsApp reminders: Edit both templates and the AV guide link.
  • Custom tab: Switch on an extra tab and write your own content.

💾 How saving works

  • Nothing is saved until you publish: Edits show on your screen right away, but only you see them.
  • Check first: 👁 Preview as leader shows exactly what leaders will see, unsaved edits included.
  • Publish: Save changes → Confirm copies the settings. In GitHub, paste them into portal-config.json and commit.
  • Everyone updates: Vercel redeploys in about a minute, then everyone sees the change after a refresh.
  • Who can publish: Anyone with write access to the GitHub repo.
  • Don’t leave mid-edit: Closing or leaving the page discards unpublished edits.

↗️ Done outside the portal

  • Bookings: In Google Calendar. Title the event “YA Cell” and add one line per room in the description, e.g. M6-1: The Dwelling. Booked rooms with no line show as Unassigned.
  • Who can open the portal: Anyone with the Vercel link. Leaders don’t need a claude.ai account.
  • Undo a change: In GitHub, open the history of portal-config.json and revert the commit, or ask Claude.
  • Bigger changes: Ask Claude for layout, new features, the passcode or new kinds of tabs.

⚠️ Hidden isn’t secret: Hiding a tab removes it from view, but its text is still in the page code. Don’t put passwords or personal details in any field here.

Tabs

Switch on the tabs leaders should see. You always see every tab here as admin. Tabs hidden from leaders are dimmed in your tab bar.

Welcome note

Shown to everyone under the tab bar. Leave empty to hide it. Use *asterisks* for bold.

Collection details (Cell Bookings tab)

Shown when a leader taps a booking. Aircon, piano and guitar lines only show when that booking needs them. Use {av_link} for the AV guide link.

WhatsApp reminders (Send Reminder tab)

Placeholders: {date} {cell} {location} {av_link}. {extras} on its own line adds the piano/guitar lines when needed. Start a line with #. to number it automatically, or - for a WhatsApp bullet.
